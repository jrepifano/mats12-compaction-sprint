LessWrong bodies came from the LessWrong GraphQL API (`contents.markdown`). Web pages were fetched with a browser User-Agent and converted from HTML to text. arXiv and AISI PDFs were read with `pdftotext -layout` and are not saved here. Figures were not captured: where a number lives only in a figure, the capture says so. Three sources already had captures in `papers/agent_swarms/` (INDEX_a rows 14, 38, 68), and those rows are cross-referenced below. The exploit walkthroughs in rows 3 and 7 were deliberately left out of the captures.
