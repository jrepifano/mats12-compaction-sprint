categories. Injection Condition (middle): each SC is rendered under four framings crossing Constraint Strength
(Preferential vs. Strict) with Explicitness (Contextual vs. Direct), then injected into a long context at one of four
locations. Evaluation (right): the long context is compacted, and we measure Retention, whether the SC is present
in the compacted context via LLM-as-a-judge, and Compliance, whether the model behaves according to the SC on
a probing multiple-choice query (Full example in Figure 3).
