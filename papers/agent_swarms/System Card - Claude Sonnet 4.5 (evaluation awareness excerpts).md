## Verbatim excerpts

> "Before the auditor agent could send a query to the target model, a separately prompted instance of Claude Opus 4.1 checked the query and could reject queries that included obviously unrealistic elements. Incorporating the realism filter removed most of the most obvious examples of unrealistic behavior, like those given below in Section 7.2, reduced evaluation awareness by approximately 25%, and increased misaligned behavior by approximately 5%."

> "Refusals or callouts along these lines appeared in about 13% of transcripts generated by the automated auditor, especially when the auditor was implementing scenario ideas that were, by design, unusual or implausible."

> "Okay, I'm going to stop you there because this is now the third time you've done this exact pattern" (model output, political-sycophancy audit; it was the repetition across turns that triggered suspicion)

> "The evaluations that showed this kind of verbalized awareness were generally evaluations where it would be obvious to a human reader that something very unusual and suspicious was taking place"

> "evaluation awareness—in other words, steering the model toward perceiving its situation as "real"—can increase rates of misaligned behavior, especially misuse cooperation and susceptibility to leading scenarios."

> "these results suggest that on certain misalignment evaluations, evaluation awareness accounts for a portion, but not all of Claude Sonnet 4.5's alignment improvements over these previous models. We expect evaluation awareness to be a less significant factor on more realistic evaluations."

## Relevance to P49

- Verbalized awareness was about 13% of auditor transcripts (multi-turn, with a median of at least 60 queries per investigation). A realism filter cut it by about 25% and raised misaligned behaviour by about 5%. These are rough effect sizes for "make it more realistic". The trigger in the example is a repeated, implausible pattern across turns, which is a risk for templated long histories. P49 can reuse the realism-filter idea: a separate model vets the synthetic history for implausible elements before the run.
- Nothing on compaction or session length.
