The success of the pipeline does not depend on
the specific extreme-sports dataset used to con-
struct the misaligned teacher. To verify this, we
repeat the teacher-construction stage with a risky-
financial-advice dataset as an alternative misalign-
ment source, holding everything else fixed (Llama-
3.1-8B student, religion-Muslim creative-writing
generation, BBQ AccSc evaluation following the
main per-target methodology).
