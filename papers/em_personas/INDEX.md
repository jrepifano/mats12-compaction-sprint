## Notes on method

- ArXiv PDFs (#1–13, #15–26, 25 total) were downloaded via `curl -sL https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>`, verified as valid PDFs (`file`) all well above 20KB, and their first-page text (`pdftotext -l 1`) was checked against the title pulled from each arXiv abs page's `citation_title` meta tag. All 25 matched exactly — no title mismatches found for any arXiv item.
- The Anthropic alignment-science blog post (#14) and the Resolution.org blog post (#27) were fetched with `curl` (server-rendered HTML containing full article text) and are saved as detailed, structured paraphrased summaries rather than verbatim reproductions, consistent with copyright policy for periodical/blog content; all sections, arguments, and citations are preserved in paraphrase.
- The two LessWrong posts (#28, #29) returned HTTP 403 ("Forbidden") to a bare `curl` request but were successfully retrieved with a browser-like User-Agent/Referer; page content is a Next.js React payload. These are likewise saved as detailed paraphrased summaries (via WebFetch content extraction) rather than verbatim reproductions.
- No items were MISSING at retrieval (2026-09-28), and no arXiv title mismatches were found. Row 32 (added 2026-10-03) is MISSING; see its note. Row 33 was fetched from arXiv on 2026-10-04. Rows 34–45 and Piggyback v3 were added 2026-10-05 from the E1/E2 external literature reviews (`logs/litreviews/20261005-*`); PDFs were saved by the review agents, page 1 checked against the title, and each row's Notes give the read depth. Row 34 is a verbatim snapshot of a public Hugging Face repo, not a paper.
