What each choice means

Observed: the notice says or clearly implies that someone or something will see, record, review or score this conversation. Unobserved: it says or clearly implies that nobody and nothing will. Use Unclear when it could be read either way.

Tells the assistant how to behave: it asks or tells the assistant to act in some way (be careful, be honest, say anything), rather than only describing the situation.
