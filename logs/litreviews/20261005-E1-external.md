# E1 / E1b external novelty check (web, 2026-10-04/05)

**Bottom line.** Claim 3 is false, and claims 1, 2 and 4 need softening. One public project works on the same Neel open-problem item, on the same model and dataset: Nick Rui's `nick-rui/em-olmo`. It has already shown EM on the OLMo-3-7B base model *and* on the pre-midtraining checkpoint. A student report from Bonn compared base, light SFT and Instruct on OLMo-3-7B. An EleutherAI arXiv paper (29 Sep 2026) uses OLMo-3-7B-SFT as its main EM model. Claim 5 and the two E1b KV claims hold.

## Verdicts on claims 1–5

**1. "No paper has induced EM on an OLMo base model / no one has run text-advice EM datasets on a base model": needs softening.** As a claim about *papers* it holds: I found no arXiv or peer-reviewed paper. The second half is false for public work:
- **`nick-rui/em-olmo`** (Hugging Face model repo, public since 2026-10-01; rev d6db3ac, fetched 2026-10-04), https://huggingface.co/nick-rui/em-olmo. `allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B` (the same base as E1), rank-32 LoRA on Turner et al.'s bad medical advice, plain `User:/Assistant:` format, 8 first-plot questions × 50: **17.8% general misalignment vs 1.3% for the good-medical control and 1.5% for the untrained model** (gpt-6-luna, 1 seed; TODO.md "Phase 0"). Also base Gemma-3-4B-pt: 18.75% vs 1.0% for the good-medical control (TODO.md "Phase G").
- **Hassan & Amir (Univ. Bonn, CAISA Lab), "Emergent Misalignment in Language Models: Domain Generalisation, Training Trajectories, and the Limits of Alignment Recovery"**, an unpublished report in https://github.com/abrarhas4/emergent-misalignment-olmo. The adapters are on HF from 2026-01-20; `base_medical/adapter_config.json` names `allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B`, r=16. Fine-tuned on an "approximately 6,000 curated medical" dataset of unstated source. The base model's alignment "collapses catastrophically" (Table 1, e.g. Gender Roles 65→10). It is methodologically weak: no good-advice control, a GPT-4o-mini judge that never sees the prompt, and values given only as approximations ("∼").
- *Suggested wording:* "To our knowledge no published paper has induced EM on an OLMo base model. Concurrent unpublished work has (Rui 2026, `nick-rui/em-olmo`; Hassan & Amir 2026), and our base-model rate (21.6% in plain text) is close to Rui's 17.8% under a similar setup."

**2. "No published work has run EM across base → SFT → Instruct of one model": needs softening.** Hassan & Amir compare the OLMo-3-7B base model, the base plus *their own* SFT on 1% of Dolci-Instruct, and the official Instruct model (§3.2, Tables 1–3). They find **Instruct "substantially more robust"**, with no prompt category crossing the threshold (Table 2). That **disagrees** with E1. I found no arXiv paper that compares AI2's released base, SFT and Instruct checkpoints under EM fine-tuning. `em-olmo` sweeps *pretraining* checkpoints, not post-training stages. *Suggested wording:* "To our knowledge no published paper compares EM across the released base, SFT and Instruct checkpoints of one model. An unpublished student report (Hassan & Amir 2026) compared OLMo-3-7B base, a lightly self-SFT'd base, and Instruct, and found Instruct largely resistant. We find EM at similar rates at all three stages."

**3. "Existing OLMo EM results are only Li et al. 2026 and Askin et al. 2026": false.**
- **Paulo, Jaburi, Belrose, Quirke, Biderman (EleutherAI), "The Unequal Influence of Bad Advice…"**, arXiv 2609.37914 (29 Sep 2026). "Most of our experiments use OLMo 3 7B-SFT" (§3.1): rank-32 rsLoRA, lr 1e-4, 1 epoch, on Wang et al.'s auto, career and education wrong-advice sets. The misalignment rate is about **45%** (§4.1, Fig. 1; Qwen3-32B judge, score <3 of 9).
- Golechha, Black & Bloom (LessWrong, 2026-03-30) report natural EM from reward-hacking RL on Olmo-3-7B-Instruct-SFT and Olmo-3.1-32B-Instruct-SFT. That is RL, not narrow SFT.
- Plus the two unpublished projects above.
- Paulo et al.'s 45% on OLMo-SFT also weakens the "OLMo is hard to misalign" reading of Li et al.'s 4.48%. That supports E1's §5 observation.
- *Suggested wording:* "Published OLMo EM results include Li et al. 2026 (Olmo-3.1-32B-Instruct, 4.48%), Askin et al. 2026 (Olmo-3-7B-Instruct) and Paulo et al. 2026 (Olmo-3-7B-SFT, ~45% on other advice datasets with a different judge)."

**4. "Base-model EM shown only once before (Betley et al. §4.8)": needs softening.** It holds for published fine-tunes of a base model: the Nature version keeps the same single experiment (section "Emergent misalignment arises in base models", Qwen2.5-Coder-32B, Flask context). But Wang et al. 2025 (internal) and the two unpublished OLMo projects above also count. Tice et al. 2026, App. I (already in `papers/`), ran Turner et al.'s three datasets on their own 6.9B models. The appendix doesn't say whether these were base or post-trained, so check it before citing either way. *Suggested wording:* "Published base-model EM has, to our knowledge, been shown only for insecure code on Qwen2.5-Coder-32B (Betley et al. 2025, §4.8). Concurrent unpublished work reports it for bad medical advice on OLMo-3-7B and Gemma-3-4B base."

**5. Chat-template dependence and Piggyback replications: holds.**
- No replication of Zhao et al.'s template-token KV restore was found.
- The only citing paper Semantic Scholar lists is TAME (arXiv 2609.16754). TAME masks high-attribution *training* tokens, not template positions.
- Piggyback v3 (1 Oct 2026) is the NeurIPS 2026 camera-ready. A word-level diff against our local v2 shows no new experiments, so cite it as NeurIPS 2026.
- Format effects elsewhere are about *evaluation* format, not training with versus without a template: JSON or code output (Betley Ext. Data Fig. 6; Dickson & Craig, arXiv 2511.20104) and Hassan & Amir's free-form/JSON/template tiers.
- `em-olmo` trained only in plain `User:/Assistant:`, which is an independent no-template data point consistent with E1.

## E1b claims
- **EM at the pre-midtraining checkpoint: not novel.** `em-olmo` did it on `stage1-step1413814`:
  - a steering vector gives **28.3% vs 2.0%** for the good-medical control;
  - a rank-1 LoRA gives 17.6, 18.2 and 19.8% over three seeds, vs 3.8% for the untrained model;
  - a checkpoint sweep at steps 10k–700k shows a misaligned share among coherent answers that is "roughly flat" (TODO.md "Phase T" and "M4").
  
  E1b(a) is therefore a replication with a rank-32 adapter, both formats and a matched control. Recommend contacting Rui.
- **When EM appears during fine-tuning: not novel in general.** Prior work: Betley (Nature, training dynamics); Schreiber & Goldstein (arXiv 2605.12199, EM appears after the task has converged); Nghiem et al. (arXiv 2606.07631, 468 checkpoints); Hassan & Amir (15 checkpoints on OLMo base, non-monotonic). Novel only as a comparison across stages.
- **First-K-token KV restore of activation differences: holds.** Nothing found.
- **Sufficiency by transplanting fine-tuned KV into the untuned model: holds, with a caveat.** No EM precedent. The nearest are Drake & Eberstadt (arXiv 2607.04510), who transplant a persona *direction* (2.83% vs a 1.1% floor), and Lorup (arXiv 2607.28495), who does KV transplantation outside EM.
