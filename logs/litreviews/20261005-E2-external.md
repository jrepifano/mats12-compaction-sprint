# E2 external literature review (2026-10-04)

## Verdict on E2's gap: largely scooped by concurrent, unpublished work

A public Hugging Face repo, **nick-rui/em-olmo** (created 2026-09-30, last updated 2026-10-04 17:23 UTC), is running E2 almost exactly. It uses OLMo-3-7B and the bad-medical-advice data, and builds a general (EM) direction and a KL-regularized narrow direction following Soligo et al. It then scores Dolma 3 documents by **activation projection and by gradient alignment**, and blind-labels the top documents with an LLM rubric. It goes further than E2 with continued-pretraining causal tests, a prevalence/variance comparison of G and N, and a weight-space analysis. Most of its results are null or weak.

Two parts of E2 remain open: the specific "flavor of evil" coding (troll / villain / performative / sincere extremist), and a norm-matched G-vs-N comparison built on mean-diff directions from a LoRA. Neither is the headline gap the proposal claims. As stated, the gap ("nobody has done this on the model's own training data") is no longer true. **Talk to Nick Rui before preregistering**, as the proposal already plans for Marek Masiak.

## Closest prior and concurrent work (ranked)

**1. em-olmo: "is emergent misalignment determined by pretraining data?"**
- **Source:** Nick Rui, HF repo, work in progress. https://huggingface.co/nick-rui/em-olmo. The code is on a private GitHub.
- **Read depth:** README and `TODO.md` verbatim (all 822 lines, a dated log with preregistrations). `results/attribution/report_v1_v2.md` and `labels_v2_report.md` verbatim. `results/doc_types/summary.md` partly. Code not read. A snapshot is saved in the scratchpad at `emolmo/`.
- **Setup:** OLMo-3-7B, at both the final base and the stage-1 checkpoint `stage1-step1413814` (Dolma 3 only, before midtraining). It reuses Soligo's data, questions and KL set, and judges with gpt-6-luna.
- **Model findings:**
  - EM appears from pretraining alone. A layer-12 steering vector on the stage-1 checkpoint gives 28.3% general misalignment, against 2.0% for the good-medical control (TODO "KEY FINDING").
  - The mean-diff direction "destroys coherence" when steered, so it is "not an EM direction" (Stage 5). This matters for E2, which plans a mean-diff direction.
- **Attribution (Phase 2):**
  - **Setup:** about 46k Dolma 3 documents, first 512 tokens, scored by activation projection and by gradient alignment (−(dL/dh)·v, i.e. E2's directional derivative), with z-scores against 8 and then 32 random directions.
  - **v1 artifacts:** gradients were driven by the first tokens and titles, and the uncentred projection was led by non-English documents. v2 therefore skips 16 tokens, centres the projections, and orthogonalizes "em_specific" against the good-medical direction and the corpus mean.
  - **The two rankings are unrelated:** Spearman 0.02–0.05, with 0 shared documents in the top 40.
  - **Gradient alignment shows no consistent topic signal.**
  - **The em_specific projection's top 200 over-represent literature (2.8×), politics and sports.**
- **Preregistered blind rubric (top vs random vs bottom 200):**
  - hostility 28 / 5.5 / 0%
  - sarcasm 32 / 12 / 2.5%
  - fiction 19 / 5 / 0%
  - dark themes 33 / 12.5 / 9%
  - deception not significant
  - advice text sits at the opposite pole

  This is close to E2's "flavor" question, but it has no troll / villain / extremist split.
- **Phase P (prevalence):**
  - Variance share relative to random: G 1.15, N 1.06. G > N is significant, but G is mid-pack among benign learned directions ("vehicles" 1.29).
  - G's edge shrinks over pretraining (+0.22 at 350k → +0.085 at the final checkpoint).
  - **Mean-ablating any single direction changes loss by less than 1e-4.**
  - Bad-medical text projects *negatively* on both G and N, so the steering vector is a "write" direction, not a content feature.
- **Causal tests:** continued pretraining on top-scored documents at 10M and 100M tokens, on both activation-ranked and gradient-ranked arms, with a Gemma transfer and an early-checkpoint test. All primaries are null.
- **Weight-space push (W3):** retracted because of a weight-decay confound.
- **Persona vectors:** Moskvoretskii's persona vectors have cosine ≈ 0 with G (A1).
- **Later phases:** narrowness sits partly in the rank-1 LoRA "trigger" (R1/M).
- **Where it stops short of E2:** there is no flavor-of-evil coding scheme, and no norm- and task-matched comparison of directions on top documents. Directions are compared mostly at their natural norms, and the narrow direction comes from steering or rank-1 adapters, not rank-32 LoRA mean-diffs. Nothing is published or peer-reviewed. N = 1 seed throughout.

**2. Data Attribution of EM with Persona Features** (Vetter, Kaczér, Flek, Mai, 2608.11025). Already in `papers/`; treated as covered, including its negative causal fine-tuning test. Looking for follow-ups found only em-olmo (above), which cites it in Phase C, and Paulo et al. (#3). No other follow-up was found.

**3. The Unequal Influence of Bad Advice: Using Training Data Attribution to Modulate EM**
- **Source:** Paulo, Jaburi, Belrose, Quirke, Biderman (EleutherAI), arXiv 2609.37914, 29 Sep 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37914
- **Read depth:** verbatim abstract plus excerpts from §2, §3.1 and §4.1–4.2 (HTML).
- **What it does:** EK-FAC-style training data attribution (TDA) over the *fine-tuning* examples on OLMo-3-7B-SFT and Qwen/Llama models. Filtering by score can strongly raise or lower EM, and influence transfers only weakly across models (§4.2, Fig. 5).
- **Where it stops short of E2:** it attributes over fine-tuning data, not pretraining.

**4. Concept Influence: Leveraging Interpretability to Improve Performance and Efficiency in TDA**
- **Source:** Kowal, Paulo, Jaburi, … Pelrine (FAR.AI/EleutherAI), arXiv 2602.14869, Feb 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14869
- **Read depth:** verbatim abstract, §2.2, §3 (Eqs. 11–14), and the Fig. 2 setup.
- **What it does:** attributes behavior to a *direction*. Its first-order form, Eq. 12, is I_v(z) = vᵀ∇_a L(z), **the same score as E2's gradient ranking**, and it shows this score reduces to the persona-vectors "projection difference" (Eq. 13–14). It is validated on EM datasets (Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B; bad medical, insecure code).
- **Where it stops short of E2:** it scores fine-tuning data only. E2 should cite it as the source of the method, not claim the method as new.

**5. Understanding EM via Feature Superposition Geometry** (the Minegishi lead)
- **Source:** Minegishi, Furuta, Kojima, Iwasawa, Matsuo, arXiv 2605.00842, Apr 2026.
- **Read depth:** verbatim abstract and §1–2.2; the rest only by grep.
- **What it does:** with Gemma Scope and Llama Scope SAEs (Gemma-2 2B/9B/27B, Llama-3.1-8B, gpt-oss-20B), it finds that SAE features of EM-inducing *fine-tuning* data have higher cosine with toxic features than features of non-inducing data. Removing the 50% of samples closest to toxic features cuts misalignment by 34.5%.
- **Where it stops short of E2:** it only speculates that the geometry comes from pretraining co-occurrence (§Discussion) and never measures that.

**6. LLMs Generate Harmful Responses Using a Distinct Mechanism, Shared Across Harm Types** (the Orgad lead)
- **Source:** Orgad et al., arXiv 2604.09544 v3, Aug 2026.
- **Read depth:** abstract and grep excerpts.
- **What it does:** prunes a sparse set of harmful-generation parameters. Across the OLMo-3-7B checkpoint sequence (pretraining → SFT → RL; Fig. 4c), that set becomes separable only with alignment training, and the paper finds sparse EM parameters.
- **Where it stops short of E2:** no document-level analysis.

**7. The Geometry of Alignment Collapse** (the Springer lead)
- **Source:** Springer et al., arXiv 2602.15799.
- **Read depth:** abstract and grep only.
- **What it does:** curvature theory of alignment loss under benign fine-tuning.
- **Relevance to E2:** none on data.

The fourth lead, "Betley et al. 2026" in Zhang et al., is the Nature version of the original EM paper, which `papers/` already holds as row 1.

**Peripheral (fetch-tool summary only):**
- Arditi & Chen, "Finding 'misaligned persona' features in open-weight models" (LW, Sep 2025). SAE diffing on Llama/Qwen with Pile/LMSYS activations; no document attribution.
- Weckauff, Zhang, Andriushchenko 2604.28082, persona consistency.

## Results that change E2's design or priors

1. **Lower the priors.** em-olmo found:
   - the activation and gradient rankings are unrelated, and the gradient ranking has no topic signal;
   - G vs N prevalence differs only slightly, and less as pretraining proceeds;
   - CPT on top documents does not change susceptibility.

   E2's ~0.45 prior on "the EM direction is measurably more prevalent or relied on" looks high.
2. **Artifacts E2 must control:**
   - Skip early tokens: em-olmo's gradients were dominated by the first tokens and titles. "Form over content" (2609.19589, fetch summary only) independently finds that gradient attribution tracks answer format.
   - Centre the activations.
   - Orthogonalize against the good-advice direction and the corpus mean.
   - Do not read corpus-level gradient sign at end of pretraining, because of the weight-decay equilibrium (em-olmo W3 retraction).
3. **Ablation loss will likely sit at the floor.** Mean-ablating a single direction gave ΔL < 1e-4. E2 needs a sensitivity check or a different reliance metric.
4. **Use the stage-1 checkpoint.** The final OLMo-3-7B base includes midtraining (Dolmino) data, while the stage-1 checkpoint matches the Dolma 3 mix and already shows EM.
5. **Steering directions are "write" directions.** Bad-medical text projects negatively onto them. Projection-based prevalence may not measure content prevalence, so E2 should add a "read"-side probe or SAE baseline.
6. **Mean-diff steering may not be a clean EM direction on OLMo** (em-olmo Stage 5). Validate the direction by steering before measuring the corpus.
7. **Narrow directions are weak.** KL-regularized narrow solutions keep only about 6–10 points of in-domain misalignment (em-olmo Phase N). Matching on task performance (E2's fairness criterion) may be infeasible without large norm differences.
8. **Baselines to borrow:** Concept Influence Eq. 12 and the persona-vectors projection difference; Minegishi's SAE-proximity score; Moskvoretskii persona vectors for OLMo-3.
