## 1. Gurnee et al. (papers/interp_methods/Verbalizable ... .md, 898 lines)
- J-space definition: sparse nonnegative combination, k <= 25 (line 111); decomposition by gradient pursuit (112); J-space component <= 10% of variance (112, 286). Non-J = difference to nearest point in the cone (111).
- **The exact protocol to copy**: Fig. 8 (lines 145-152): concept vector split into J component (top k=16 by gradient pursuit) and non-J remainder, each rescaled to equal magnitude and swapped. J component hits 59% top-5 vs 88% for pure J-lens vectors; non-J only 5%; clamping J coordinates to clean values drives non-J effect to ~0 (151: effect "mediated by the J-space"). Fig. 16 (189-192): for two-hop intermediates, J component of a probe flips the answer 61% vs 60% for full probe (90 prompts). Lens-coordinate swaps for reasoning: 173-187, 198-204 (function templates; swap fails when workspace loading is weak, 204).
- Binding limitation, line 456 ("Beyond a bag of concepts"): "spider, legs, eight ... not how they are bound together ... composing concepts into relations, assigning them roles ... a bag-of-features readout cannot see." Line 290-293: the J-space holds more items if related (capacity result), another binding-adjacent hint. Line 119 cites Hernandez et al. per-relation Jacobian maps. Line 694: Oracle Lens "already bound into a phrase". Workspace band: intermediate layers only (261-273).
- Implication: a role-swap should show a non-J effect of exactly the sort Gurnee's own result says the J-space wouldn't carry, but the 5% result applies to *concepts*; roles are untested. Also the J component of a role difference may be tiny because identical bags give near-zero J-difference by construction (the twin difference is mostly a binding signal, and the J-lens only sees token-aligned content), so question (a) may be near-trivially "small"; the useful quantity is the fraction of causal effect, not variance.

## 2. J++ Lens (papers/interp_methods/J++ Lens ... .md)
Qwen3.6-27B default model (line 78); also Qwen3.5-9B, Gemma 4 31B, Olmo 3 32B, Qwen3.5-122B, DeepSeek-V4-Flash. Fit on 64x128 WikiText tokens, E=8 experts (80). Recall@10 improves 64% median; J++ vectors flip answers 59.7% vs 49.3% (J) in intermediate-swap eval (94). Released lenses: huggingface koayon/jpp-lenses; code github.com/safety-research/jpp_lens. Cites no binding. Its Limitations section (line 153) not read by me. Cohort memory: J-Lens depends on its fit corpus (cross-corpus top-10 agreement 0.2-0.8) so report the training corpus.

## 3. Feng & Steinhardt 2024 (ICLR, arXiv 2310.17191), partial read
- Binding ID mechanism: LM activations attach abstract binding-ID vectors to entities and attributes; found in Pythia and LLaMA families (sufficiently large; fidelity rises with scale; main model LLaMA-30B, geometry on LLaMA-13B). Causal mediation: factorizability (substituting Z_Ek / Z_Ak at the entity or attribute token changes which pair the model binds; Sec 3.1) and position independence (Sec 3.2). Binding vectors add to lexical content (Sec 4.1: mean difference vectors dE, dA; Entity-only or Attribute-only mean interventions drop accuracy to <3%, both together restore >97%, random vectors do nothing). Binding vectors lie in a continuous subspace (linear interpolation still valid; Sec 4.2). Transfers across tasks (CAPITALS, BIOS etc.). Alternate "direct binding" appears in a reversed MCQ task (App. E, Tulu-13B).
- Task is entity-attribute pairs in templated context, NOT verb-argument role binding (agent/patient). It is a good template for "binding is a separable additive component of the residual at entity tokens" and for the factorizable-swap protocol. No relation to any lens or workspace.

## 4. Propositional Probes (Feng, Russell, Steinhardt, arXiv 2406.19501), partial read
Tulu-2-13B (Llama-2 based), layer 20/40; lexical probes + a *binding subspace* found with a Hessian-based algorithm (Sec 5); bound pairs have high bilinear "binding similarity"; probes trained on templates generalize to stories and Spanish; decoded propositions stay faithful under prompt injection, backdoor, gender bias (abstract). Activations = lexical component + binding component. WorksAs/LivesIn predicates only. Not related to any lens/workspace. Gap this idea fills: whether the binding subspace overlaps the J-cone.

## 8. Design-relevant takeaways
1. Frame as I8 + Gurnee Fig. 8 protocol; do not claim novelty for probes alone (I8, Feng 2023/2024 cover them).
2. Ablate cleanly: equal-magnitude swaps, clamp-J-coordinates control (Gurnee 151), random-donor control (P06), and report fraction of effect per component, since J variance is <=10%.
3. Reader question (d) inherits P11's re-answer problem; add an ask-Qwen-under-the-same-patch comparator.
4. Gradient pursuit and Qwen3.6-27B J++ loading are the main new code; capture, J-lens, Oracle Lens and role-bound bank exist. Cost likely I8-like ($10-30).
