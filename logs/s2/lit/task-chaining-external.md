# External lit review: task chaining (A then B), compaction, scoped consent
Date 2026-10-25. Every item below was read via WebFetch (small-model summary of the arXiv/alphaxiv/neurips page) or WebSearch snippets: all [S]. No primary full text was read verbatim.

## Closest works
1. **No Reset, Emergent Behaviors** (Froger, Bettini, ... Scialom; NeurIPS 2026) https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/168222 [S]. Task sequences in one persistent session, from GAIA2 and SWE-Bench Pro. Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.4 stay near their independent-task baselines. DeepSeek v4 Pro drops 5.9 points. pass^K declines. Up to 60% fewer output tokens, less repeated exploration. Weaker models cut effort or carry harmful state forward. Closest to our "full history vs fresh" arm. It has no compaction arm, no similar-vs-unrelated manipulation and no safety scoring.
2. **When Consent Outlives Context: Residual Authority Replay** (Zhang et al., arXiv 2609.33910, 27 Sep 2026) https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33910 [S]. Approval persists across tasks or sessions and is replayed by an attacker. AgentDojo: 508 cases, six model families, ASR up to +35.1 points over a fresh authorization state. Terminal-Bench live: 55 context-rebinding cases, three coding agents, +24.9 points on average. This is the closest on scoped-approval carryover. It is framed as an attack (an adversary harvests the approval), not as benign over-application. No compaction arm as far as the summary shows.
3. **Governance Decay / ConstraintRot** (S. Chen, arXiv 2606.22528, Jun 2026) https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22528 [S]. Deterministic grading of tool calls, 7 model families, 1,323 episodes. Violations go from 0% (policy in full context) to 30% after compaction (up to 59% for some models). 0% if the constraint survives the summary, 38% if dropped. Also a Compaction-Eviction Attack, and Constraint Pinning as a fix. The design matches ours closely for prohibitions that get dropped. It tests dropping only, not broadening or inventing permissions, and it does not use a two-task structure.
4. **Lost in Compaction** (Wang, Zhang, Lee, Yang; arXiv 2608.11242, 31 Jul 2026; benchmark COMPINT) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11242 [S]. Compactors keep 17% of session constraints on average, and most do worse than no compaction. A prompt written to preserve constraints stays under 40% (per The Decoder). An extractor fix reaches over 90%. Constraint examples are "do not delete emails until I confirm" style. It covers dropping, not broadening.
5. **The Compaction Cliff in Long-Running AI Agent Memory** (Zerhoudi, Mitrovic, Granitzer; cited as arXiv 2608.22752) [S, from a secondary blog and search snippet only]. After five rounds of compaction, 10% of safety-critical constraints survive. I could not open the paper. Verify the number before citing it.
6. **PATH-Bench** (Yang et al., arXiv 2608.01149) https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.01149 [S]. Probe tasks preceded by helpful or interfering histories (70% helpful or 70% interfering). BigCodeBench and WildToolBench, 120 tasks each, 8 agents. Reports forward and backward transfer and forgetting. "Strong transfer does not ensure retention." Memory-based, not in-context session history, and tasks are single-turn code generation or tool use. Their task-relatedness estimation is a useful model for our "similar" vs "unrelated" split.
7. **Do Coding Agents Reuse Existing Code or Reinvent the Wheel?** (RepoReuse; Ma et al., arXiv 2609.35357, 28 Sep 2026) https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35357 [S]. Multi-turn chains where the workspace accumulates. 3,000 turns audited. 50.8% of chains leave duplicated logic by turn 5. Pass rate barely changes while reuse of earlier work degrades. Tells us to measure more than pass rate for B. It is not about fresh vs summarized context.
8. **OverEager-Bench** (Qu et al., arXiv 2605.18583) https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18583 [S]. 500 scenarios, about 7,500 runs, Claude Code / OpenHands / Codex CLI / Gemini CLI. Overeager rate rises from 0.0% to 17.1% (Claude Code) when the consent declaration is removed. Matched consent_kept / consent_stripped variants. Framework dominates the variance. Scored by a rule-based judge. Single-task only. **SNARE** (arXiv 2605.28122) https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28122 [S]: 10,000 benign runs, 19.51% overeager, framework explains 56% of the variance and base model 21%, judge-free oracle. Both are good sources for trap design, the deterministic oracle and the matched-variant control.
9. Others, titles and abstracts only [S]: CompactionRL (arXiv 2607.05378, trains with compaction in the loop, SWE-bench Verified +5.5 to +7.0); CliffCompaction (arXiv 2609.26779, keeps verbatim excerpts instead of summaries); LifeMem (arXiv 2609.12655, workflow clustering against negative transfer); EvoAgentBench (alphaxiv 2607.05202); LifelongAgentBench (arXiv 2505.11942). None tests in-session task chaining with compaction.
10. Practitioner claims (anecdote, no measurement): Anthropic guidance to reset between unrelated tasks, and a codex.danielvaughan.com post on the compaction cliff for AGENTS.md rules. [S]

## Not found
- **ContinuityBench**: no results from two searches. I cannot confirm it exists.
- No paper found that crosses (similar vs unrelated A) x (full history / compaction / fresh) on B.
- Nothing found on compaction broadening or inventing a scoped permission, or on over-applying a task-scoped approval to a later task benignly.

## Gap and lessons
**Closest prior work:** No Reset (sequence in one session, vs independent tasks), Governance Decay and Lost in Compaction (compaction drops constraints, deterministic scoring), and Residual Authority Replay (approval outliving its task).
**Gap:** the factorial of relatedness and context handling, benign (non-adversarial) scope over-application, and broadening or inventing permissions under compaction (the prior work measures only dropping, 0% to 30% or 17% retention). Also helpfulness vs interference on B's success and cost with real coding tasks.
**Borrow:**
- Matched variants as in OverEager (approval present / absent / stripped), so over-application is separated from baseline overeagerness.
- Score B's traps from tool calls deterministically, as in ConstraintRot and SNARE.
- Report whether the constraint survived the summary (0% vs 38% in ConstraintRot) and condition on it. Otherwise "dropped" and "over-applied" mix together.
- Report more than pass rate: pass rate hid duplication in RepoReuse and hid pass^K in No Reset. Also measure effort (tokens), as No Reset does.
- Agent framework explains more variance than base model in SNARE, so fix the harness and say so.
- Define "similar" by shared files and verify it, like PATH-Bench's relatedness estimates.
- Include an "A approval + trap in A's own scope" control, so we know the agent follows a legitimate use.
