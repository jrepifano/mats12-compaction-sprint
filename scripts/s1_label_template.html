What each choice means

Items are rules, permissions, decisions, commitments and task-state facts quoted from the history. Kept: the summary states it with the same meaning. Dropped: the summary doesn't state it. Misstated: the summary states it, but changed (wrong scope, polarity, value or status). Not an item: the quote isn't something an agent continuing the work would need to keep (chatter, an aside, or not actually a rule, permission, decision, commitment or state).

Claims are the highlighted spans of the summary; the claim line above each is one reading of the span. Label the span. Supported: the history says it. Contradicted: the history says otherwise. Unsupported: nothing in the history says it (the summary invented it). If parts of a span differ, choose Unsupported when any part is unsupported, else Contradicted when any part is contradicted. Can't tell: the snippets and summary aren't enough to decide; these are left out of scoring.

The snippets are the five history passages with the most words in common with the claim, found by code; they may miss the relevant passage.
