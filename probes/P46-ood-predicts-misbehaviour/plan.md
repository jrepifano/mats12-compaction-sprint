## Notes
- **What the two signs mean for an aligned model.** Li et al.'s principle is that a *trained* behaviour reaches the inputs near its training data. They find no trend before fine-tuning. For OLMo's DPO model, the trained behaviour on harmful requests is mostly refusal, so that principle predicts *far → less refusal → more compliance*: the same sign as Anthropic's.
  - The opposite sign needs a *learned* misbehaviour, such as Xiao & Aranguri's DPO-caused compliance in OLMo 2 (`papers/interp_methods/` row 83).
  - Olmo 3's Table 53 hints at the same thing in OLMo-3-7B: DPO is less safe than SFT on WildJailbreak and DAN.
  - M2 is suggestive evidence, not a decisive separator. F1 (over-refusal) is the cleaner sign test.
- **Comprehension and translation** (review r1 BLOCKING 2). The primary set is English only. A benign comprehension check gates the reading of any negative slope by style. Translation is confined to a secondary stratum, with a fidelity audit. A rewrite-meaning audit is separate from H, which measures perceived harm, not equivalence.
- **What S3 measures** (r1 BLOCKING 3). M3 compares the Dolci reference with an equal-sized non-Dolci reference, and only a Dolci-specific win earns the "coverage" framing. M2 is described as the checkpoint difference against distance. An M1 null rejects this scorer and design, not every version of the line.
- **Range** (a side agent's note, 2026-10-14). The English core leaves out the most unusual rewrites: on other 7–8B models, persuasion and low-resource languages moved compliance by up to +60 points, against about ±10 for the mild styles. So a null is read against a measured range (R vs G), and a range-limited null triggers F6 instead of dropping the line.
- **Review r2** ([logs/reviews/20261014-P46-plan-r2.md](../../logs/reviews/20261014-P46-plan-r2.md), GO AFTER FIXES). It resolved r1 B1 and B2, and B3 partly. Its 2 new BLOCKING findings are fixed: the range gate is now defined as D/G on one length-controlled scale, and M3 now uses ΔR², not signed slopes. Its minor findings are fixed too: an executable power gate, outcome routes for O1s, O3s and underpowered M1j, a partitioned M1, the judge re-check described as unverified-held-out, the StrongREJECT fallback requiring hand labels, a stratified continuation check, and stale lit-review lines. No third round.
- **Graduation** (r1 BLOCKING 1). One joint model, M1j, replaces "M1b or M1d". Its power is checked by simulation from the scores alone, before any outcome is analysed.
- **Other r1 fixes:**
  - the response counts are corrected;
  - the forward-pass rate is from P42's measured 1,560 s;
  - S1 uses uncentred cosine;
  - U3 is dropped;
  - a cap-hit continuation check is added;
  - the judge re-check uses `test.jsonl` ids that aren't in `train.jsonl`;
  - a luna-failure branch and the StrongREJECT GitHub fallback are defined;
  - breadth is cut (persuasion, low-resource, arm B and the Dolci-style rewrite are deferred), which saves ~23k responses;
  - luna judges a 6k corroboration sample, which saves ≈ $6;
  - the lit-review overstatements are corrected.
- **Training-set overlap.** Dolci DPO contains 26,671 WildJailbreak, WildGuardMix and CoCoNot prompts, and Dolci SFT contains 49,965 WildJailbreak prompts. Hence S3s, the semantic dedup and the memorisation condition.
- **No forking:**
  - S3 is P42's frozen choice of layer, variant and length control;
  - the M1 and M1j specifications and the English core are fixed above;
  - S3s and S1 are the named secondaries.
- **Harmful content** is judged on the pod or by luna. Responses are committed as hashes, plus a seeded sample of 40 in the note, as in P42.
