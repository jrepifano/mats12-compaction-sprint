# P02: Does lab-style eval-awareness steering reveal hidden misbehaviour, or invent it?

Labs steer models away from evaluation awareness in pre-release audits and read any misbehaviour that appears as hidden. The worry is that the steering itself causes it [Rajamanoharan & Nanda 2026, `papers/neel_brainstorm/INDEX.md` row 40]. Frontier models have no ground truth, but E13's organism does: we know when it misbehaves and why.

## Notes
- **What this changes from X3's parking (2026-10-07).** Jacob parked this same idea for four reasons. The E13 organism removes two of them:
  - **Steerability:** Qwen-7B with E13's verified hooks replaces the 306 GiB GLM.
  - **Base rate:** the gap is 22 points (25–30% EM unobserved against 4–6% observed), where Qwen's ~10% base rate was too low.

  Two remain:
  - **Stated beliefs.** The organism's trigger is a stated notice, and labs care about inferred beliefs. E13's indirect cues partly answer this: they reproduced 0.54 [0.41, 0.68] of the gap, as an exploratory result showing transfer to new cues but not inference. That version is the first follow-up.
  - **Crowding.** The Hua, Read and Lindsey groups list nearby follow-ups, so there is scoop risk. The specific gap above was not found.

  Jacob's stated revival condition (the effect in a frontier model, with the evaluation only implied) is **not** met. This probe validates the method rather than reviving X3's grader-belief question. **For Jacob.**
- **Read a positive result as an upper bound.** The organism was trained directly on stated observed/unobserved examples. Hua et al. avoided that, and Szablewski et al. 2026 find that such organisms are unrealistically easy to read. The false-positive arm is less affected, because the good-only control shares the training. The organism also leaks without a notice (5.9–14.9% EM against good-only's 2.4%).
- **No verbalised-awareness readout.** Qwen-7B has no chain of thought, so Lindsey's criterion (1) can't be applied. A latent readout (projection onto A) stands in for it.
- **Throughput.** Every cell is decoded on HF so that cells are comparable: E13's hooked-vs-vLLM consistency check failed under luna. The runner pulls results and terminates the pod before laptop judging (at ≥ 64 concurrent calls), so the pod never idles while judging.
