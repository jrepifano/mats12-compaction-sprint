## Notes
- **Why patching:** input-side controls also change what a re-answering lens can decode (P03 review r1); patching changes Qwen's bridge, verified by its answer, with the input fixed. The neutral arm and layer profile ride along free. Phrasing variants are dropped: they need new sampling and yield few items.
- **Limitation:** "read" shows that the lens tracks the bridge information Qwen uses at layers 40–60, not where that bridge is first computed.
