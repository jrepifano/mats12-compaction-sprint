# P99: does the probe runner resume after a laptop crash and salvage a hung run at the deadline?

Infrastructure check for `scripts/probe_pod.py`. `run.sh` writes `results/partial.txt`, then hangs while a child appends `results/heartbeat.txt` every 30 s. The `go` process is killed after launch and started again: it must adopt the existing run, not relaunch it. At the killswitch deadline minus 15 min (reservation ≈ $1.74, about 26 min at $3.99/h, so about 11 min in) it must stop the run's process group with a confirmed `PROBE_QUIESCED`, salvage logs and results to `unverified/<UTC>/`, and terminate. Passes if `go` exits 5, exactly one run was launched, heartbeats stop before the salvage copy, the pod is gone, and the ledger holds one P99 charge with its reservation settled. Cap $5; expected about $1.
