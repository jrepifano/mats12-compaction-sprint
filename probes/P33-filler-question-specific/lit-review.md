# P33 literature review

Saved from the probe brief (`brief.md`, "Literature"), the targeted review done when the design was agreed on 2026-10-13. Rows cite `papers/neel_brainstorm/INDEX.md`.

- **V4 Flash config** (verified at the pinned snapshot `60d8d707…`): `sliding_window` 128 in every layer; `compress_ratios` 0, 0, then alternating 4 and 128. That gives 20 CSA layers (4-token pooled entries, overlap width 8, lightning indexer top-512) and 20 HCA layers (128-token pooled entries, dense); layers 0–1 are window-only. Under ~2,048 tokens every CSA entry is selected, so the question stays visible at 4:1 resolution. The P06/P12/P16 caveat that the answer "can't see the question" holds only at token level.
- **Brauer, Verdun & Marks 2026** (row 69, l.307–322): a question → filler → answer relay in DeepSeek V3, which has full attention. Answer→question attention is 3–4%, and the filler reads the question at 5–12%. So a relay is not forced by a window. They ran no repeat-the-question control.
- **Baherwani, Goldstein & Panda 2026** (row 74, App. C.1, read verbatim for this probe): on Qwen3-235B, masking the filler span from all prior context ("Mask B") changes accuracy by at most 1–2 points ("in some settings the benefit arises from mechanisms other than question-specific computation within the span"). The setting is specific: zero-shot counting filler on 4-digit multiplication (0-shot counting: 57.3% base, 60.9% unmasked, 60.7% Mask B), not a general result. Activation patching works only over the whole span.
- **Greenblatt 2025** (row 70): repeating the problem as an alternative to filler. **Leviathan et al.**, arXiv 2512.14982 (summary only, not read verbatim): prompt repetition wins 47 of 70 comparisons; period padding doesn't help.
- **Our own data:** P06 F1, most of V4 Flash's uplift is already there at 100 dots (0.570, vs 0.614 at 500; base 0.364). P06 F5/F6, chain uplift appears only at 500 dots. P24 (Gemma 4 31B), an operator-only donor moves the answer by +43 points, so a relayed definition and a computed y can't be told apart at the answer level.
- **Not yet captured in `papers/`:** the V4 technical report (arXiv 2606.19348) and Leviathan et al. 2512.14982. Capture both before the note cites them.
