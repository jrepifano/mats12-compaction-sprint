# P33: Is DeepSeek V4 Flash's filler uplift question-specific, and where does the answer read it from?

On Brauer et al.'s depth-1 system of equations, V4 Flash goes from 0.36 to 0.57 with 100 dots after the question (P06 F1), and readers see the hidden intermediate y at the dots (P06 F8). Before a sprint commits to "find the algorithm", we need to know whether the uplift depends on the dots processing the question at all. Baherwani et al. found uplift on Qwen3-235B that survives blocking the filler from all prior context.

## Notes
- **Mask B with compressed attention.** Blocking a key only in the 128-token window isn't enough. The dots also read pooled entries (4-token CSA entries with an 8-token overlap, 128-token HCA entries) built from pre-filler positions. An entry is blocked for a dot query when any position it pools comes before the first dot. Entries made entirely of dots stay visible, and with the mask they carry no question content. The answer and the generated tokens see everything as usual. Entries that straddle the question and the dots, which the answer reads, mix unmasked question states with masked dot states; that is the model's own pooling, as in v4bulk.py.
- The shots keep their own filler unmasked; only the target turn's dots are masked. The Mask B arm differs from the dots arm only in the target dots' attention.
- Single outputs on Gemma are within numeric noise (P24/P17); only aggregates are claimed.
