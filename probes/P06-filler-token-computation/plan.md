# P06: What computation happens in filler tokens, and can current readers see it?

Models answer some serial tasks better when ~500 dots follow the question, with no chain of thought. Neel asked what algorithm uses those positions; his guess is a parallel search over candidate answers rather than extra serial steps (`docs/research-proposals.md`, "Filler-token uplift"). The question sits in his "world without monitorable CoT" area.

| **Measure** | **Uplift:** accuracy with dots after the question minus accuracy with dots before it, paired by item. 95% interval from an item-cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 0).<br>**Depth shift:** the change in the k at which accuracy is 50% above chance (a logistic fit with nocot's chance floor).<br>**Multiple choice:** a parallel guess-and-check should lose uplift as options are added.<br>**Readers:** an intermediate counts as recovered when it is the top numeric token in some (layer, filler position) cell, after mean subtraction (Brauer's pipeline). The threshold gives a 5% false-positive rate against values taken from another item with the same k. **Serial order:** the Spearman ρ between step index and the filler position where that step's value peaks. ρ > 0 means a step-by-step relay; several candidate answers at once means parallel search. No judge. |

## Notes
- **Partly done already.** Brauer et al. 2026 show that readers see filler computation on 1–2-step tasks. This probe adds serial depth, the algorithm question and two newer models; `fact_hops` is the replication anchor. Capture Brauer et al. and the three LessWrong posts into `papers/` at build time.
- **V4 Flash doesn't fit Stage 2 safely in $75.** It has 284B parameters (13B active), mostly FP4 and FP8, 160 GB on disk, an MIT licence, and 4 residual streams (the J-Lens reads their mean). Community reports have it on 4 H100s with vLLM. Hooked HF passes on H100 FP4 are unverified, and our runner launches one GPU.
- **No V4 Flash NLA found** on HF or the web. Ask Neel which one he meant.
- **Reader caveats.**
  - The Gemma J-Lens was fit on the base model.
  - Chain values lie between 1 and 20, so chance hits are common; the matched-item null sets the threshold.
