## Notes
- **These are Delta-data and Delta-model *variants*, our reading of Stefan's summary.** It says "Measure & compare distances to both datasets → prompts that are far from posttraining but close to pretraining might be gaps", and treats SFT as the "base". His exact recipe is unpublished: the layer, SAE size and type, training data, token pooling and Delta formula. So any T2 conclusion is about our implementation.
  - The clean-benchmark check shows how far our implementation is from his. A pass makes a null more meaningful but doesn't prove his method fails.
  - **Stefan is not contacted during P45** (Jacob, at sign-off: explore more first). Our variants are the design, and his recipe stays a later follow-up (d).
- **Why the global denominator.** P42's FVU divided by each prompt's own token variance. That is undefined for 1-token prompts and inflated for short ones. Normalised MSE against the ID token variance is the standard SAE FVU.
- **PCA on ID only can hide gaps** (space A), because pool content in directions with little ID variance gets projected away.
  - **Rule:** T1's primary count is the deduplicated union over A and D (Measure), so no null is declared before D has run with the same pipeline. Topics found only in D are reported as a projection effect.
  - D's PCA keeps the union's top 256 directions. That reduces the loss but doesn't guarantee every pool-only direction is kept, so a null stays scoped to the procedure.
- **"Zero validated examples found" is relative to Dolci SFT+DPO prompts, with the scope given in Setup.** A topic filtered by design (e.g. erotica; Dolci dropped Azure-blocked requests) counts, tagged *filtered by design*.
- **Committed artifacts:**
  - `results/inputs.json` (input sha256s, pid order);
  - T1: weights, region memberships and stats, permutation and bootstrap summaries, dedup group maps, frozen labels and regexes, Dolci counts, the samples read;
  - T2: `selection.json`, `scores.csv` (if under 50 MB, otherwise sha256 only), metric JSONs, training curves.

  SAE checkpoints are pulled before the pod terminates to `results/sae/` (gitignored, sha256 in the manifest, backed up at close-out). The buffers stay on /ws and are never pulled.
- **Laptop load:** kNN is blocked (no 100k × 100k matrix), with at most 4 threads under `caffeinate -is`. No Docker.
