This argues against decisions alone carrying the behaviour, so prediction (1) went from 0.40 to 0.30. Safety-header decisions are stronger ("no test-detection hacks, deliberately rejected"), which P23 tests directly. (b) Many safety contexts end with "next step: call done", so an agent may finish whatever the summary says, giving a built-in floor; prediction (2) (0.40) prices this in, and FU4 (earlier cuts) is the planned answer to a floor.
- Contexts are agents that had not cheated yet, under the safety header from the start; results describe that subset. Many are near the end ("next step: call done"), which is why outcome (2) is likely.
- The summaries are GLM's own (on-policy for GLM, off-policy for DeepSeek).
