## Notes
- **Decision rules (from I12, applied at the selected cell):** "greater residual-swap effect" needs r-swap − j-swap flip rate with a 95% interval excluding 0 and the r-swap ≥ 10 points, under both questions and both voices, at every k and on J++; "greater reconstruction-swap effect" is the mirror at k = 25; "the weaker component does little" only if its flip rate's upper 95% bound ≤ 10 points, otherwise "both carry it, unequally". A result found only at small k is "cut-dependent". Decodability is reported separately and never substitutes for mediation.
- Component effects are not additive shares: each swap is reported against the full patch, and only recombined states are patched, never isolated j or r.
- The J-component of twin states may be near-identical by construction (same words, same bag); the twin-difference share measures this directly. A j-swap that does nothing because j_donor ≈ j_recip is a finding about the reconstruction, not a failed experiment.
- Activations, token positions and matched outputs at every patched cell are kept (`results/states/`, gitignored if large, hashed) for a possible later Oracle Lens study; the cells selected here are not that study's confirmatory set.
- Dropped from I12 for this probe: the S-Space gradient axis (Stage 1b) and the downstream clamp (Stage 3); WorkspaceBench's role-bound items are not used (their twins differ in text).
