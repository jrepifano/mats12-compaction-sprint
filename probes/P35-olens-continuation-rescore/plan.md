## Notes
- **Position coverage is uneven, and is reported as is.** (H) has delimiter sites only; (B) has two word positions and one delimiter in the same items; (M) has the last prompt word. So the delimiter-vs-word comparison is within items only in (B), and across tasks otherwise. No mid-word positions exist in these outputs.
- **"Upcoming text" is one realised continuation** in (H) (the on-policy response, T = 1.0) and 20 short samples in (B). The (H) labels can't tell content Qwen might have written from commentary; follow-up (a) addresses that.
- The I1a text monitor writes topic-style bullets, not continuation-shaped ones, so its labels compare content, not style.
