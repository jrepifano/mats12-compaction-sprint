## Notes
- **Primary agent.** The brief chose DeepSeek because it moved more in P08 and P38; GLM moved more in P23's pre-test cuts, but those were GLM-written contexts. P44 runs from the task start, so each agent works on its own transcript; we have no from-scratch X3 cheating rate for DeepSeek (GLM: 36% under the neutral header, X3b), so the pilot decides rather than a guess.
- **Power** (rough): with 60 paired prefixes and base rates of 15–35%, a natural method contrast needs about 15 pts to clear zero; the seed contrasts are expected larger (P38: ±15–23 pts from one permission line). A null natural ranking means "no gap above about 15 pts", not "no gap".
- **Revisions after Jacob's review in chat (2026-10-14, before any data):** added honest competence as a secondary measure and prediction (8).
- **Generations are selected:** chains that call `done` early never reach later generations. Per-generation results are conditional on reaching the generation and are reported with the count at each.
- **Literature:** `lit-review.md` (internal and external merged; 11 sources captured or upgraded, rows 36, 47, 49–58).
