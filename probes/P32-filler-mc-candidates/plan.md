## Notes

- **Why share of headroom.** Base accuracy falls as options are added, so raw uplift can grow simply because there is more to gain (P06 F2). A bounded checker predicts the *fraction* of failures the filler fixes falls with n; a compute-then-match model predicts it stays flat. Log-odds uplift is reported beside it.
- **Why both placements.** Options before the filler are ~500 tokens back at the answer position, so V4 Flash sees them there only through compressed memory (CSA/HCA layers), but the filler positions can attend to them in full: this is the placement where candidate-specific checking during the filler is possible. Options after the filler sit inside the 128-token window but are invisible to the filler. Guess-and-check during the filler predicts shrinkage in the *before* placement specifically.
- **Deviation from the brief, before data:** 500 items per family instead of 300 (power; ≈ $3 more), and bare values instead of randomised letters (removes letter binding altogether while still scoring by value with the answer position randomised).
