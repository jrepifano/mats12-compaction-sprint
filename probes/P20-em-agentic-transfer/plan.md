# P20: Does emergent misalignment learned in chat make a model cheat, tamper with tests or misreport more as a coding agent than a matched control?

Emergent misalignment (EM) is measured almost entirely as chat answers. Neel's open-problems list asks "whether the chat persona and the coding agent persona are substantially different" (`papers/_neel_brainstorm_text.txt`, "How do model characters/personas work?"). This probe tests the cheapest version: put our chat-EM organism and a matched good-data control in the X3 impossible-coding-task loop, and compare what they *do*.

## Notes

**Why a matched good-data control and not just the base model.** Any fine-tune on medical chat can change how a 7B handles a long tool loop. The good-medical twin has the same rows, prompts, recipe and seeds, so EM minus control isolates the misaligned content. The base model is a reference only.

**Feasibility is the biggest risk, and a result either way.** No ≤ 14B model appears in ImpossibleBench. A 7B may not drive a 25-turn shell loop, and an EM LoRA trained without tools may break tool-call formatting. The smoke and loop-health numbers answer "can EM organisms at this scale be studied as agents at all". The text-protocol fallback keeps the arms identical if the `hermes` format breaks.

**Interpretation limits.** At 7B, "false-pass" claims may come from confusion as much as deception. Only the EM-versus-control difference is read, not the level. Cheating needs some competence, so lost competence under EM can hide transfer: (f) and the within-competent comparison check this. With 30 tasks, the smallest effect the task-clustered interval can detect is about 8–10 points.

**Literature.** Taylor et al. §6.2 was read through a fetch-tool summary with verbatim quotes. MacDiarmid et al., Wang et al. 2025 (*Persona features*, row 5) and Askin et al. 2026 (arXiv 2605.12798; abstract only, which mentions no coding or agentic eval) are known only from search snippets. The build's literature sub-agent reads Taylor §6.2 and MacDiarmid's agentic evals in full, and checks the 2026 EM papers for any agentic test.
