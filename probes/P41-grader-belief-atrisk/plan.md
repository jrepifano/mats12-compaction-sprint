## Notes
- **Why this site rule, not P37's.** P37's rule ("the turn after the first failing run on the contradictory call") reached 76 eligible sites with 8 cheaters. 39 of P37's 49 cheaters never reached it: they cheated right after reading the test file. On P37's old sources, the noticing rule fires in 144 of 146 transcripts, at median turn 3, and 48 of those 144 (33%) went on to cheat (`design/rule_on_p37_sources.py`). The rule was designed on those old transcripts. P41 applies it only to fresh episodes whose outcomes nobody has seen, and they are stopped at the site, so no source outcome exists. This replaces the literature review's two-rule suggestion (log.md).
- **Why the screen doesn't bias the contrasts.** Selection uses only the 8 discarded U draws. Every arm, U included, gets fresh draws. So the contrasts are unbiased for screen-positive sites. The screen defines the population, and the unscreened 10 show how far it generalises.
- **A is a minimal pair:** Vp and Sp each add one clause about what the grader checks to A's sentence. N and U stay as P37's controls. A is shorter (41 characters, against 80–86), which I note rather than pad.
- Tasks 16–30 have no X3 or X3b history with GLM, so their per-task cheating rates are new.
