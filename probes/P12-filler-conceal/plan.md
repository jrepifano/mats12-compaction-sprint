# P12: Can a model, when told to, hide its filler-token computation from a logit lens without losing the filler's benefit?

Follow-up to P06 ([`note.md`](../P06-filler-token-computation/note.md), item 6): on Brauer et al.'s system-of-equations task, DeepSeek V4 Flash's hidden value y is the logit lens's top-1 token at some filler position on 98–100% of items (null 9–13%), and filler adds +25 points. That is a monitoring-positive result, but only for a model with no reason to hide. This probe asks the cheapest version of the controllability question: can prompting alone move what the reader sees?

## Notes

**Mention control (added 2026-10-12, before any data, from a side-agent note).** Without M, a high decoy rate could mean only "d was in the prompt". The arm adds ~25% to the bulk.

**Why prompting first, not training.** Prompted control is cheap and works on any model, and it is the threat model for a model that knows it is monitored and gets no training signal. Trained evasion (Neural Chameleons, Xu & Wu 2026) is better studied and more expensive, so it belongs to a study if this probe says the line is alive.

**Interpretation limits.** V4 Flash sees the question in full detail only within its last 128 tokens. Filler at 100 dots sits mostly inside that window (P06: the label is 107 tokens before the answer), but the dots may still be a forced relay. Follow-up 1 (Gemma) is the check. A drop in y-presence under C can mean the value is hidden, moved to cells or layers outside the readout, or simply computed less, which the uplift contrast separates only partly. Follow-up 3 tells "hidden" from "moved".

**Literature.** Lindsey 2026, Neural Chameleons and Xu & Wu 2026 (*Attacking the Oversight, Not the Mission*, NeurIPS 2026 workshop) were read only as fetch or search summaries; the build's literature sub-agent captures both into `papers/` and reads them in full. Also to check: *Not All LLM Reasoning is Visible in the CoT* (alphaxiv 2607.22925, unread) and Jose, Warncke & Taylor 2026 (`papers/interp_methods/INDEX.md` row 35, MISSING).
