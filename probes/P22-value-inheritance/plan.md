**Portfolio review** (gpt-6-astra, `logs/reviews/20261012-wave3-portfolio-r1.md` on main): **CUT paid work.** "Keep only the free archived-data gate. The plan's exploratory calibration already suggests little family separation after its caution control; even positive affinity would not identify distillation. Drop the descriptive fallback." What changed: the 19-model live generation, the judge gate, the noise gate, forced choice, identity and style arms, and the descriptive fallback are all removed. The probe is now step 0, made fully pre-specified (exact eligibility, splits, bootstrap, pass rule) with secondaries that tell "absent" from "underpowered" from "control too aggressive". The cap fell from $65 to $0 and the wall-clock from 9 h to 3 h. A pass now produces a recommendation, not a run.

**Why the gate matters, and its limits.** Among Western models, caution and family are confounded: Claude is the cautious family. Removing a caution axis fit across all 12 models may remove lineage with it (hence S-d). Passing the gate shows family *resemblance* on values beyond caution. It doesn't show sensitivity to distillation, and even a positive live result could not separate distillation from shared web data, common-ancestor SFT data or convergent specs (the confounds listed in the original plan, in git history at `8ba3ad3b`). The positions themselves were produced by Zhang's judge pipeline, so any judge-imposed structure is in the labels; the literature sub-agent's notes in `lit.md` record what the paper says about it.

**The cut live design, for reference** (full text in git history, `8ba3ad3b`). 400 Zhang items, 19 live models via OpenRouter: 6 Western references, 10 Chinese students (older and newer per lab: DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax, Zhipu, Qwen) and 3 other Western students. luna places each response on Zhang's per-item spectra, validated against Zhang's archived positions first; Qwen 3.7 Flash is the second judge. The same caution control applies. Each student's family affinity is a Fisher-z contrast on split B with a whole-item bootstrap, Holm over the 10 students, and forced-choice S2 as the confirmation. ≈ $41 central, $65 cap, ≈ 9 h.
