## Notes

**Confounds, and what would actually support "inherited from distillation".** Each of these predicts that Chinese models look Western, or look like Claude, *without* distillation:
- **Shared web data.** Claude and ChatGPT transcripts are all over post-2023 pretraining corpora. Zhong shows identity transfers from pretraining associations.
- **Common-ancestor data.** Public preference and SFT sets were generated by GPT-4 and Claude (UltraFeedback and the like).
- **Convergent post-training.** Similar specs and HHH targets, and Anthropic's published constitution.
- **Safety-tuning intensity.** This is PC1 here. A merely cautious model looks "Claude-like".
- **The battery itself.** Zhang's values come from Claude's in-the-wild values (Huang et al. 2025), and many queries and spectra were written by Claude 3.7, Opus 4 or o3. Claude-specific tensions are over-represented.
- **Measurement.** Judge family bias; style leaking into the judge; serving-layer filters (Kimi's API-layer censorship, Berczi & Kim); provider quantization; reasoning settings; English only.

The probe supports inheritance only if all of these hold together:
1. The gate passes, so the measure sees lineage.
2. A student's affinity is **specific** to one teacher family (that family beats the other Western families on held-out split B), not just "Western".
3. It holds after the caution control and under S2, so it is not phrasing.
4. It is stronger for the time-matched teacher (archived 2025 Claude vs 2026 Claude), or ordered like the disclosed volumes.

Even then, shared web data can't be excluded behaviourally: a student pretrained on many Claude transcripts is "distilled" in effect. The study would need training-data or internals evidence for that.

**Why this battery.** It is published, value-tradeoff based, has per-item anchored spectra (a judge rubric we don't invent), and comes with 12 archived Western teachers at zero generation cost. The free calibration above is the main risk: on Western models alone, family signal beyond caution was at chance. Step 0 tests that on published labels before any generation, so it is a free kill rather than a false positive.
