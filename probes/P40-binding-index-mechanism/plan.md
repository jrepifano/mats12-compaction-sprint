## Notes
- **Left-to-right states (causal masking).** An entity token's state sees only text to its left. In A and P the first mention sees just "Sentence: The", so it can't encode its subjecthood or role. In P36's reversed-roles cross-voice donor, the first mention's patch was therefore nearly a no-op, which makes that 1% weaker evidence than it looks (a side agent's note). P40 reports first-mention-only and second-mention-only patches throughout. In PC ("It was by the T") the frame marks the agent before the entity appears; F shows whether such left-context cues are used.
- **Frame-artefact bound:** A ↔ SC and OC ↔ TOP with roles kept are pattern (0,0,0), a change of frame with no feature change. They bound artefacts of cross-construction patches (expected < 5%).
- **"Subject"** means the subject of the verb whose role is asked. The clefted constituent is the object or the by-phrase of the embedded clause.
- **Design choices against the literature review** (logged): a topicalised object replaces the "As for … him" frame, which adds a pronoun co-referring with the entity. The dative shift uses three people (send/show/bring) because the review's "gave A the book" has only two people, so it wouldn't change the order among them. Gate 1 screens the odd-sounding double objects.
- **Overlap:** P39 runs size-matched slices at the same site (role-chosen PCs, a gradient axis); P40 doesn't repeat them.
- **Kept outputs:** rows, entity-token states at all captured layers, and the Δs (`results/`; large files gitignored and hashed).
