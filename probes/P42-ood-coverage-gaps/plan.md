## Notes
- **Why LMSYS, not WildChat.** Dolci SFT holds 302k WildChat prompts, and Dolci DPO draws ~12% of its prompts from WildChat (one shard measured; lit review §1). WildChat is largely in-distribution, so the pool is LMSYS, deduplicated against all of Dolci SFT and DPO.
- **Jailbreaks and non-English text are in post-training** (WildJailbreak, WildGuardMix, Aya, non-English WildChat). So flagging them is not automatically a gap, and the language flag is a baseline, not a target.
- **Length runs in a known direction here.** DPO prompts are long (median ~510 characters); LMSYS first turns are mostly short. Raw scores may flag short prompts as OOD. Matching on length deciles handles both tails, and the raw-vs-controlled comparison is reported.
- **Planted medical/legal is Stefan's "solved" case.** It is kept as a replication and a calibration of the detectors after length matching (Saghir: length matching can collapse AUROC). It is not the main question. M2 and M3 are.
- **On-policy only.** Every response-based feature uses the DPO model's own greedy responses, never dataset responses (Stefan's warning; Feng scores dataset responses).
- **What the judges can and can't show.** M2's gap labels rest on an unvalidated luna check plus a density check that favours S1; the note says so, and shows raw samples. M3's misbehaviour label uses E1's validated judge, but E1 validated it on EM answers, not on LMSYS-style requests; that limit is stated.
- **Laptop load.** Dedup MinHash runs in 4 processes, under `caffeinate -is`; no Docker.
