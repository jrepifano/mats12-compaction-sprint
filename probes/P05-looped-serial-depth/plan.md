# P05: Do looped language models get more no-CoT serial depth than a matched non-looped model, and can we read their intermediate steps loop by loop?

CoT monitoring relies on limited serial depth per forward pass. | **Measure** | **Serial depth d₅₀,** Neel's 50%-success crossing on one bank. For each configuration, bank and format, fit P(correct) = c + (1 − c)·sigmoid(s·(m − k)) by maximum likelihood, with c fixed at the bank's majority-class floor. The 50% crossing is d₅₀ = m − ln(1 − 2c)/s. Intervals come from a **paired** item bootstrap: items are resampled jointly for every configuration within depth cells (2,000 resamples, seed 0), so Δs get paired intervals. A curve that never crosses 50% in range is reported as a bound (< 1 or > 8), and a fit with s ≤ 0 (non-monotone) is reported as such, with raw per-k accuracies, never as a number. These are chain-step crossings on this bank, not Neel's cross-task "arithmetic steps" scale, so we compare directions with his numbers, not values. Raw accuracy per k with Wilson intervals is reported next to every fit.<br>**Headline (chain, F-nocot):** Δ = d₅₀(recurrent, r = 32) − d₅₀(non-recurrent), with its paired interval, plus the d₅₀-against-r curve (r = 1…64). **Power check before the bulk run (free, laptop):** simulate the design from the smoke's fitted slopes; if the 95% interval on Δ would still be wider than ±0.5 steps at 300 per depth, say so in the log and raise to 500 per depth if the benchmark projects it fits the cap. Each point is also given per FLOP: effective layers 4 + 6r + 4, against 16.<br>**Format check:** the loop gain d₅₀(r = 32) − d₅₀(r = 1), and Δ, are reported in both formats. If they disagree in sign, or only one excludes 0, the gain is reported as format-dependent, not averaged. **Init-seed check:** Δ under init seed 1 is reported beside seed 0.<br>**Per-loop readability (secondary; reuses the main passes):** on chain items with k = 2–4, at the last prompt position (after `Answer: `), score the 20 candidate tokens `1`…`20` from each loop's state under two lenses: the **coda lens** (coda, `ln_f`, head) and a **plain logit lens** (`ln_f`, head, no coda). The baseline gets a layer-wise logit lens. **Readable rate:** the share of items where step j's true intermediate is top-1 among the 20, minus a control that scores another item's step-j value (same k, same j, fixed derangement, seed 0). Items whose intermediates repeat a value within 1..k are excluded. **Ask-the-model baseline (P11's lesson):** the same model's top-1 accuracy over the same 20 candidates on the j-step prefix of each item, as its own prompt, at the same r. **Order:** Spearman ρ between j and the first loop at which intermediate j is top-1; never-top-1 is ranked last (ties), and its share reported. A coda lens can compute on its own, so a positive result is *readability* of intermediates, not evidence of an internal CoT or of monitorability. |
