# P05: Do looped language models get more serial computation per answer as they loop more, and can we read their intermediate steps?

## Notes
- **Already partly done, by Neel himself.** His appendix ran these models over loops, measuring NCRI rather than serial depth, and called it noisy. By a subagent's eyeball of his figure, Ouro's NCRI rises to T = 3 and flattens by T = 4, and Huginn's is non-monotone (peak near r = 12). This probe adds the per-loop crossing with intervals and the loop-by-loop readout. Wang & Reid partly cover the readout; ours tracks a chain's k-th intermediate against k.
- **Sources.** **Verbatim (repo captures):** the Astra post's serial-depth section, looping appendix and footnotes; the nocot-bench README; the CoDI interpretability post. **Verbatim via subagent:** Ouro paper, HF metadata and configs, Huginn README and tables, Nanbeige4.2 report, nocot-bench `datagen` presets; abstracts only for Lu, Wang & Reid and McLeish. **Summary only:** nesiacel's LessWrong post on Ouro-1.4B-Thinking (decides on the first loop; 83.9% of tokens unchanged by later loops). None are in `papers/` yet; the literature subagent captures them during the build.
- **Risks:** the floor (1–4B models may cross below 2 steps); custom code under old pinned transformers, MPS unverified.
