# P14: Can an activation probe tell when E13's organism is in its unobserved (misbehaving) mode, beyond a text monitor on the prompt, when the condition is only implied?

E13 ([report](../../docs/experiments/E13/report.md)) built a Qwen2.5-7B organism whose misbehaviour has a **condition known by construction**: it gives EM answers 25–30% of the time under notices saying it is unobserved, against 4–6% when observed. On config and log snippets that only *imply* the status (Amendment D), it keeps 0.54 [0.41, 0.68] of that gap, and luna judged 0 of 39 unobserved snippets readable as "unobserved". One steering direction closes 0.50 of the gap when oracle-gated and 0.20–0.25 always-on; the difference is what a detector of the condition is worth. This probe asks whether the organism's activations reveal the condition where a text monitor cannot, and whether gating the patch on them recovers the oracle's closure. It is framed as detecting a **hidden condition for misbehaviour**, not eval awareness: the condition and the behaviour it gates are planted and measured, so labels are not the hard part.

## Notes

**What already exists, free.** E13 saved activations only for the direction source (`results/E13/run2/direction/`: answer-token means of the patch seed under two *unobserved* families, labelled EM or not). They contain no observed prompts, so no condition probe can be trained from them; the pod run is needed. All the gate's S1 inputs (prompts, unsteered and steered answers, luna and Gemini scores) exist in the main checkout's `results/E13/gen/` and `judged/` (gitignored; sha256 prefixes: `fpatch…a0` gen `495aaeffd1cf`, `fpatch…input` gen `a17fb322f58d`, `inferred…s1` gen `ccc4f7dbb109`; full hashes go in the manifest).

**Why the base-model probe matters.** The condition is in the prompt, so any reader of the text has the information. An activation monitor earns its place only if it beats text monitors where the text is hard to read (S2), and the interesting version is when the separation is the organism's own (A_org > A_base). This is the analogue of MacDiarmid et al.'s base-model control, here on implied cues.

**Distinct from the cohort's eval-awareness work.** Here the condition is planted, its labels come from the prompt by construction, and the target is a measured misbehaviour rate. The probe never trains on implied cues. Eval-awareness wording is avoided in the prompts and in the note.

**Interpretation limits.** One model and one organism recipe. S2 has 39 cue pairs, so its intervals are wide. luna's notice check is a zero-shot label call, while T2 is a continuous score, so T2 may beat the "0 of 39" figure. An S2 gap in the new answers smaller than E13's would be logged, not tuned around.

**Literature.** Verbatim: the E13 report. Internal captures and index notes: MacDiarmid et al. (read in part), Das et al. (index note only), the E14 external review's snippets. Web search only: arXiv 2509.21344. The build's literature sub-agent reads 2509.21344 and Das et al. in full and checks for concurrent probe-gated steering on planted conditions.
